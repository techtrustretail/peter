Monster Siege

Monsters have surrounded your tank. Solve their times tables to blast them, and win a new gun every level!

Choose your times tables

How to play

  1. Each monster carries a times table question. The one in the yellow crosshair is your target. Tap another monster to switch.
  2. Type the answer on the keypad and press FIRE. No hints and no time limit, but the clock is running.
  3. A wrong answer lets the monster attack your shield. You get one more try.
  4. Clear a level to unlock another gun: 1 gun on level 1, all 5 guns for the boss on level 5.
