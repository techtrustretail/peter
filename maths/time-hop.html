Stormy lives in the Mint Chocolate world at the North Pole.
Help Stormy hop through the hours of the day and night — and win ice-cream scoops! 🍦

👪 For parents: this game fixes the mistake “9 pm → 4 am = 17 hours”. The right way is to count forward through midnight: 9 pm → 12 midnight = 3, then 12 midnight → 4 am = 4, so the answer is 7.
