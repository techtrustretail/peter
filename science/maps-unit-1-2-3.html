Unit 1
Living Things

My body, animals
& staying healthy

Cơ thể, động vật & sức khoẻ

1🦴 Bones & skeleton

  • A skeleton = a strong frame of bones that supports the body from the inside. (bộ xương)
  • Humans have 206 bones. Bones are hard, strong and light.
💀 skull sọ😬 jaw hàm🐍 spine cột sống🫁 rib cage lồng ngực🦵 hip hông💪 arm bones🦿 leg / thigh bones
  • A model shows how something looks or works. It is not the same as the real thing.

2🛡️ 4 jobs of the skeleton

  • Protect organs: skull → brain 🧠, rib cage → heart ❤️ & lungs 🫁
  • Support the body & give it shape
  • Help us move (muscles pull on bones)
  • Help us grow — we grow because our skeleton grows. An adult's skeleton has stopped growing.
👶5 cm →🧒11 cm →👧18 cm →🧑bigger!
  • Measure in cm (standard unit), not hand lengths — hands are different sizes!

3💪 Muscles & movement

  • Muscles pull bones (they never push).
  • Muscles work in pairs.
contracts corelaxes duỗi
gets shorter & fatter → pulls the bonegets longer & thinner → goes back to normal size
💪Arm bends: top muscle contracts, bottom muscle relaxes
  • Moving keeps us healthy: stronger heart, lungs, muscles & bones, good mood 😊

4🐟🦗 Different skeletons

  • Vertebrates = have a backbone (skeleton inside) (có xương sống)
    🐟 fish – scales🐸 amphibians – wet skin🦎 reptiles – dry scales🦅 birds – feathers🐱 mammals – fur
  • Invertebrates = no backbone (không xương sống)
    • Exoskeleton = hard skin/shell on the outside: 🦗 locust, 🪲 beetle, 🦀 crab, 🕷️ spider
    • Soft body: 🪱 worm, 🪼 jellyfish
  • Identification key = yes/no questions to name an animal.

5💊 Medicines & germs

  • Medicines make us better or prevent illness.
  • Take safely: only from a doctor, nurse or your adult · follow the instructions · the right amount · never a friend's medicine.
  • Ways: 💧 liquid/tablets · 💉 injection · 🌬️ inhaler · 🧴 cream · 🏥 drip
  • Infectious diseases are caused by tiny living things called germs. People 🧑, animals 🐔 and plants 🌾 can all get them (flu, measles, bird flu, leaf blast).
  • Vaccination = an injection that stops you getting a disease.
🐱

Stormy's memory tricks

  • Contract = Crush shorter · Relax = Return longer
  • Vertebrate = has vertebrae (backbone) · Invertebrate = no backbone
  • Exo = outside (like a knight's armour 🛡️)
  • Skull → brain · Rib cage → heart & lungs
  • Germs → infectious disease · Vaccination → prevents it
  • Bigger person → longer bones (the skeleton grows)

Unit 2
Energy

Energy makes things
move or change

Năng lượng làm vật chuyển động hoặc thay đổi

1🌈 Energy around us

Forms of energy (các dạng năng lượng):

💡 light🔥 heat🔊 sound🏃 movement🔌 electrical
  • The Sun ☀️ is a source of energy — it is not a form of energy.
  • We cannot make energy. Energy can only move (transfer) or change form.
  • All living things need energy.

2➡️ Energy transfers

  • Transfer = energy moves from one thing to another. (truyền năng lượng)
  • An energy chain shows where energy goes:
☀️Sun →👕wet clothes →dry!
♨️hot water →🥄spoon →jelly →🔴bead falls
  • Some heat always escapes to the surroundings → hot water cools down.
  • Kicking a ball ⚽ and hitting a drum 🥁 = transfers. A log just lying there = not a transfer.

3🔄 Energy changes

Energy can change form when it moves:

4🌿 Energy & living things

  • Plants use the Sun's energy to make food → producer.
  • Animals eat to get energy → consumers.
☀️→🌿plant →🐇rabbit →🦅eagle
  • Arrow → means "is eaten by" / energy goes to.
🐇 herbivore – plants🦅 carnivore – animals🐻 omnivore – both🐯 predator – hunts🦌 prey – is hunted

Unit 3
Materials

Everything is made of
tiny particles

Mọi thứ đều được tạo từ hạt rất nhỏ

1🧱 Matter, materials, particles

  • Matter = everything around us.
  • Material = a type of matter, usually a mixture: wood, glass, plastic, rubber.
  • Substance = one pure thing: salt 🧂, pure water 💧, oxygen.
  • Particle = a very tiny bit. All matter is made of particles, and they are always moving. (hạt)
💧food dye spreads in still water → particles move!

2🔵 The particle model

SolidLiquid
packed tightly, regular patternclose, no pattern
very small spacesbigger spaces
wiggle in a fixed place → keeps its shapeslide past each other → flows, takes the shape of its container

3🏖️ Solids that pour

  • Powders — sand, flour, salt, sugar — are solids that can pour like a liquid.
  • They are made of tiny grains with air (space) between them, so the grains can move and flow.
  • Each grain is still a solid made of millions of particles.
  • Reliable results = repeat the test and get the same answer. Use seconds (standard unit) to time it.

4🍫 Melting & solidifying

🧊solid — heat 🔥 → melting → liquid💧
💧liquid — cool ❄️ → solidifying → solid🧊
  • Solidifying is also called freezing. (đông đặc)
  • Heating gives particles more energy → they move faster and further apart.
  • This is a change of state = a physical process: still the same substance, and we can change it back.
  • 🍦 Ice cream mix (liquid) in the freezer → solid ice cream. Chocolate, butter and even metals melt.

5🧪 Chemical reactions

  • Substances react and make a NEW substance. Usually we cannot change it back.
  • 🔥 Burning wood → ash + gases
  • 🧱 Cement + sand + water → concrete
  • 🔩 Rust: iron/steel + water + air → reddish-brown rust. Rust makes metal weak (cars, bridges).
  • Stop rust: paint 🎨, oil or grease 🛢️ (keeps air and water away).
🐱

Stormy's memory tricks

  • Solid = soldiers in a line 💂 · Liquid = kids at playtime 🏃
  • Melt = solid → liquid (needs heat) · Solidify = liquid → solid
  • Change of state = same stuff, new shape ↩️ can undo
  • Chemical reaction = new stuff ✨ can't undo
  • Rust needs Water + Air → "rust needs a WAsh"
  • Flour pours but it is still a solid!
