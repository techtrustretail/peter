Lila & Stormy Bedtime Stories

Lila & Stormy: Two Bedtime Stories

Two ways the story could continue. Each one borrows from a story you already know. Read one each night, then play “Story Detective” at the end.

Night 1: The Lamp Night 2: The Tiny Door
Night 1 · borrows from Aladdin

Lila and the Lamp of Whiskers

About 6 minutes to read aloud. Do the voices!

Page 1

The door creaks open

Lila turned the rusty key. Clunk! The old door groaned open, and a cloud of dusty, spicy air puffed out into her face.

Inside was a room glittering with treasure: piles of gold coins, jars as tall as Lila, and silk cushions in every colour of the market. In the middle, on top of a golden lamp, sat a chubby grey cat. It was fast asleep and snoring very softly.

Lila“Hello?” she whispered.

One sleepy eye opened. Then the other.

Stormy“Oh! A visitor!” purred the cat, stretching its tiny paws. “I'm Stormy, Keeper of the Lamp. You may look at anything you like… but please don't touch the gold. The gold here is very grumpy.”

Lila giggled. She had never met a talking cat before, and she liked this one already.

Page 2

Uh-oh: Mr Grabbit

Suddenly, a shadow fell across the floor. A tall man in a purple robe and an enormous orange turban stood in the doorway. It was Mr Grabbit, the carpet seller who had been shouting “Rare treasures!” in the market.

Mr Grabbit“So, little girl, you found my secret door!” he sneered, twirling his curly moustache. “I have been looking for that lamp for forty years.”

He snatched the golden lamp right from under Stormy. Then he stepped outside and SLAM! The door shut. Click! The key turned in the lock.

Everything went pitch-black.

Lila“We're locked in!” Lila gasped, hugging her map tightly.

Stormy“Don't worry,” said a small voice beside her ankle. “Mr Grabbit took the lamp. But he forgot the most important thing.”

Lila“What?”

Stormy“Me.”

Page 3

The secret of the lamp

In the dark, Stormy began to glow, softly at first, then brighter and brighter, like a warm golden moon.

Stormy“That lamp was never magic,” Stormy explained. “It was just my bed. I'm the magic one. But my magic only works when somebody asks nicely.”

Lila thought for a moment. Then she knelt down and stroked Stormy's soft, silky ears.

Lila“Stormy,” she said politely, “please could you open the door?”

Stormy closed its eyes and began to purr. Rrrrrrr… The purr grew louder and deeper until the whole room trembled. The lock rattled. Then POP! The door burst open.

Outside, Mr Grabbit was running away through the market with the lamp under his arm. But Stormy gave one more tiny purr, and the colourful carpet he was running across lifted up into the air, with Mr Grabbit sitting on it!

Mr Grabbit“Heeeelp! Put me dooooown!” he wailed, as the carpet zoomed over the rooftops and far away.

Page 4

One wish

When everything was quiet again, Stormy sat down in front of Lila and wrapped its tail neatly around its paws.

Stormy“You were kind to me,” it said. “So you get one wish. You can have all the gold in this room, if you like.”

Lila looked at the shining coins. She looked at the jars and the silk cushions. Then she looked at Stormy, who had been alone in that dusty room for a very, very long time.

Lila“I don't want the gold,” she said. “I wish that you could be free, and come and see the world with me.”

Stormy's whiskers twitched. Its eyes went shiny. Then, with a happy little hop, it jumped straight into Lila's backpack.

Stormy“Wish granted,” it purred. “Now… do you have any snacks?”

And as the sun went down over the busy market, Lila walked home with the best treasure of all: a brand new friend.

The End.

Night 2 · borrows from Alice in Wonderland

Lila and the Tiny Golden Door

About 6 minutes to read aloud. The Queen needs a very loud, very cross voice!

Page 1

Down, down, down

Lila turned the rusty key and stepped through the door. But there was no floor! She began to fall, down, down, down a long tunnel lined with colourful carpets. Strangely, she fell slowly, like a feather, so it wasn't scary at all.

Lila“Whoaaaa!” she squealed. “Where am I going?”

Bump! She landed softly on a big purple cushion. In front of her was a tiny golden door, no bigger than a teacup. Next to it sat a plump grey cat holding a plate with one round cookie on it. The cookie had pink icing that said EAT ME.

Stormy“Welcome, welcome!” purred the cat. “I'm Stormy. If you want to go through the tiny door, you have to eat the cookie.” Then it added in a small voice, “I already ate the other eleven.”

Page 2

The Queen of Spices

Lila took one bite. Fizz… whoosh! She shrank, and shrank, and shrank, until she was as small as a date. Stormy shrank too. (It was still chubby. Just smaller.)

They squeezed through the tiny golden door into an enormous garden. The flowers were as tall as trees, and the air smelled of cinnamon, ginger and pepper.

But waiting for them was a big, round, very cross peppercorn wearing a golden crown. It was the Queen of Spices, and behind her stood a row of peppercorn guards.

The Queen“THIEF!” bellowed the Queen, pointing at Lila's map. “That is MY map! Guards, off with their whiskers!”

Lila“But I don't even have whiskers!” Lila protested.

Stormy“I do,” squeaked Stormy, hiding behind her. “And I'm very fond of them.”

Page 3

A very clever sneeze

The guards marched closer. Stomp, stomp, stomp. Lila's heart was thumping. Then she noticed something: there was a pile of fine brown powder by her feet. It smelled of pepper.

Lila“Stormy,” she whispered, “what happens when a peppercorn smells pepper?”

Stormy's eyes went wide. “Oh, you clever girl.”

Lila scooped up a handful of powder and blew it into the air. A brown, spicy cloud floated over the guards, and…

Everyone“Ah… ah… AH-CHOOOO!”

The guards sneezed so hard they rolled backwards like marbles. Even the Queen's crown flew off her head! Lila and Stormy ran as fast as they could back to the tiny golden door.

But oh no, Stormy had eaten one cookie too many. It was stuck halfway! Lila pushed and pushed, until POP! Stormy shot through the door like a fluffy cork.

Page 4

Was it a dream?

“Fresh dates! Beautiful rugs!” Lila blinked. She was sitting on a colourful carpet in the middle of the busy market. The sun was still high in the sky. The old wooden door was gone.

Lila“It was only a dream,” she sighed. “What a shame. I liked that cat.”

She unrolled her map to find her way home… and froze. Right in the middle of the map was a small, grey paw print. And next to it, a few pink cookie crumbs.

Then, from inside her backpack, came a soft and very happy sound.

Stormy“Purrrrrrr.”

Lila smiled the biggest smile of her life. Maybe, just maybe, it wasn't a dream after all.

The End.
