Peter's Story Nights

Peter's Story Nights: Lila meets Stormy

The Story Continues… · 4 nights · 10 minutes a night · Mum asks, Peter imagines

Every idea is a good idea Talk first, write last Ask, don't fix Stuck? Offer two choices Peter can answer in Vietnamese
Night 1

Behind the Door

Imagine the place and meet the cat.

Say:"Lila turns the rusty key… click! The door creaks open. Guess who is sitting inside? A chubby grey cat…"

Ask, one by one
  • What is Stormy doing when Lila walks in?
  • What can Lila see, hear and smell?
  • What are Stormy's very first words? Say it in Stormy's voice!

Write down: 3–5 key words + Stormy's first line = speech #1.

Night 2

Uh-oh!

Find the problem and Stormy's special power.

Say:"Everything was fine until… CRASH! What happened?"

Stuck? Let Peter pick one
  • The door slams shut and locks them inside.
  • A cheeky mouse steals the map.
  • Stormy vanishes, leaving tiny paw prints.

Ask:"Stormy is too chubby to run fast. So what special power does it have?"

Role-play 2 min: Peter = Stormy, Mum = Lila. Catch one great line = speech #2.

Night 3

The Twist

A surprise, an ending, and four little drawings.

Ask:"Hmm… who drew Lila's map in the first place?"

Twist ideas, only if Peter needs them
  • Stormy drew the map to find a friend.
  • The rusty key is really Stormy's collar bell.
  • The treasure is… a giant jar of cookies!

Ask:"Does Lila take Stormy home, or does Stormy stay to guard the door?"

Peter's job: draw 4 comic boxes: door opens · problem · rescue · ending.

Night 4

Pictures to Paragraphs

One drawing becomes one paragraph.

Peter: writes one paragraph for each picture, in part 4 of the worksheet.

Mum's 10 minutes: upgrade 2–3 sentences by asking
  • "How did Stormy move?" (waddled, tiptoed, rolled)
  • "Did Lila say it, whisper it or shout it?"
  • "What did the room smell like right then?"

Check together: problem + solution + ending · 2 speech lines in “ ” · wow words · 4 paragraphs.

If Peter gets excited and talks for longer than 10 minutes, let him. That is the best sign of all.
